03
Unified Clearances

Applicable departments review the application

After BPLO initial review, the application is routed simultaneously or sequentially to the municipal offices applicable to the business.

location_city MPDC / Zoning architecture Engineering / OBO health_and_safety Health / Sanitation eco MENRO / Environmental real_estate_agent Municipal Assessor home_work Barangay Clearance storefront Market Office
verified Important: Only the departments applicable to the business are included in the review. Some offices may conduct physical inspections or request additional compliance documents before endorsement.
low_priority Sequencing: Locational and zoning clearance is conducted prior to Engineering / OBO building safety evaluations.
04
Bureau of Fire Protection

BFP endorsement and fire safety processing

local_fire_department EXTERNAL AGENCY PROCESS

After the applicable municipal reviews are completed, the application proceeds to the Bureau of Fire Protection for the required fire safety process and endorsement.

Applicants may be required to complete BFP inspection, submit fire safety requirements, or comply with BFP instructions before the application can proceed.

policy National Agency Coordination: The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) is a national agency; fire safety evaluations and physical inspections are coordinated directly with the Victoria Fire Station.
receipt_long Fire safety processing fees and Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) release are administered under BFP statutory guidelines and cannot be paid directly through e-Tech.
05
Local Revenue Code

Assessment is prepared

calculate Tax Order of Payment (TOP)

Once the required municipal reviews and BFP processing are completed, the applicable permit fees and statutory assessment are prepared.

account_balance

Assessment computation depends on declared business classification, gross sales/capital investment, regulatory clearances, sanitary inspections, garbage fees, and municipal tax schedules. A formal Tax Order of Payment (TOP) is generated for Treasury settlement.

06
Municipal Treasury

Proceed to Treasury

payments EXTERNAL MUNICIPAL PROCESS

The applicant proceeds through the Municipal Treasury process for statutory payment and official receipt issuance.

account_balance_wallet
Settlement Policy Notice

Payment is handled outside e-Tech. Municipal taxes, regulatory clearance fees, and statutory charges are settled directly at the Municipal Treasury Cashier (Victoria Municipal Hall) or designated official municipal payment facilities. Online credit cards, GCash, and digital e-wallets are not collected through this portal.

07
Credential Issuance

Business permit is released

Final Release Milestone

After the required approvals and payment confirmation are completed, BPLO processes the business permit for official release.

qr_code_2 Secure QR Validation Anti-counterfeiting token

Released permits include an authenticated municipal QR code that can be scanned by the public and inspecting officers to verify validity.

Best Practices

Keep your application moving.

Three practical reminders to ensure quick and smooth processing under R.A. 11032 statutory timelines.

document_scanner
Reminder 01

Upload clear and correct documents

Legible scans of business registration (DTI/SEC/CDA), barangay clearances, and proof of address prevent BPLO deficiency holds and instant resubmission requests.

check_circle High-res PDF or JPG only
notifications_active
Reminder 02

Check your application for remarks

Log in regularly to view evaluator feedback, scheduled site inspection notices, or requested document revisions from municipal engineering, zoning, or sanitation units.

notifications SMS & Email alerts active
hourglass_top
Reminder 03

Complete external requirements promptly

Attend to BFP fire safety instructions and proceed to the Municipal Treasury cashier upon assessment release to avoid municipal surcharge assessments or statutory delays.

priority_high Avoid late surcharges
