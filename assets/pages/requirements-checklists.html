REGULATORY CLEARANCES & INSPECTIONS

Additional Requirements Based on Your Business

Some businesses require specialized clearances, zoning assessments, or safety certificates depending on business activity, physical premises, or hazardous materials.

check Zoning / Locational Clearance

Conformity certification that the commercial operation aligns with Victoria's Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP).

check Vicinity / Location Map

Clear map sketch identifying street landmarks, adjacent commercial establishments, and ingress/egress roads.

check Proof of Property Authority

Copy of Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT), Tax Declaration, or notarized Contract of Lease with owner's consent.

check Site Development Plan (If Applicable)

Required for duck farms, hatcheries, warehouses, agro-industrial plants, and high-density commercial centers.

check Certificate of Occupancy

Official certificate certifying that the structure has been fully inspected and complies with the National Building Code (PD 1096).

check Building Permit & Ancillary Clearances

Required for newly constructed, renovated, expanded, or altered commercial tenant spaces.

check Electrical & Mechanical Inspection

Signed plans and annual certification for heavy commercial loads, motor pumps, generators, or cold storage.

check Signboard & Billboards Permit

Standard municipal clearance for outdoor signages, facade signboards, and promotional street canopies.

check Annual Sanitary Permit

Issued following on-site sanitary inspection of business premises, pest control, and food storage facilities.

check Employee Health Cards

Individual medical clearances (chest X-ray, stool exam, hepatitis screening) for all food handlers and active crew.

check Water Potability Bacteriological Test

Accredited laboratory analysis certifying drinking and washing water safety for food preparation spaces.

check Grease Trap & Pest Management Contract

Required for food establishments, bakeries, car wash stations, and commercial kitchens.

check Solid Waste Management Plan

Documented program for segregation, recycling, and disposal complying with Republic Act 9003.

check LLDA Clearance / Discharge Permit

Laguna Lake Development Authority clearance for facilities producing commercial wastewater or lake proximity activities.

check DENR ECC or Certificate of Non-Coverage (CNC)

Environmental Compliance Certificate or CNC for enterprises categorized under environmental impact monitoring.

check Updated Tax Declaration (TD)

Certified copy of current Tax Declaration for land and commercial building improvements in Victoria.

check Real Property Tax (RPT) Clearance

Proof of updated real property tax settlement issued by the Municipal Treasurer's Office.

check Machinery & Equipment Declaration

Itemized schedule of operating machineries, industrial cold storage, and manufacturing assemblies.

check Barangay Business Clearance

Endorsement from the Punong Barangay of the jurisdiction where your establishment operates.

check Barangay Official Payment Receipt

Proof of local community clearance fee payment, attached to the municipal licensing docket.

check Barangay Certificate of Non-Operation (For Retirement)

Attestation confirming complete cessation of retail or commercial trade in the barangay premises.

check Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)

Statutory certificate confirming fire suppression, egress routes, and emergency alarms adhere to RA 9514.

check Fire Extinguisher Official Receipt / Tag

Valid inspection tags and supplier receipts for compliant chemical or CO2 fire extinguishers.

check Fire Safety Maintenance Report (FSMR)

Mandatory for multi-story buildings, resort properties, event venues, and educational institutions.

check Market Stall Award Notice

Official certification of stall allocation issued by the Victoria Municipal Market Committee.

check Contract of Stall Lease

Active formal lease contract signed with the Local Government of Victoria, Laguna.

check Market Master Clearance

Certification that stall rental payments and municipal utility dues are paid up to date.
