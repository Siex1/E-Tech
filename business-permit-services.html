  • sync_saved_locally Select your active Victoria Business Identification Number (BIN) from your verified dashboard.

04 PERMANENT CESSATION

Close or retire a registered business.

For business owners requesting the lawful closure, cessation of commercial operations, or retirement of an active business record in the Municipality of Victoria.

Official Cessation Workflow

  • verified Submit an electronic Business Retirement Request via e-Tech with the registered business record.
  • verified Upload the Notarized Affidavit of Closure specifying the exact date of cessation and legal grounds.
  • verified Upload Barangay Certificate of Business Closure issued by the local barangay where operations ceased.
  • verified Undergo statutory Municipal Treasury assessment to settle any unpaid municipal tax obligations and surcharges.
  • verified Final official Certificate of Business Retirement is signed and released following verified clearance.
warning

Treasury Assessment Notice: Gross receipts realized up to the date of cessation are assessed pursuant to the Victoria Municipal Revenue Code. Outstanding balances, if any, must be fully satisfied at the Municipal Treasury prior to issuance of the final Certificate of Retirement.

Request Business Closure arrow_forward View Closure Requirements arrow_outward
gavel Statutory Cessation Mandate
30-Day Mandatory Filing Window

Under the Local Government Code (R.A. 7160), business owners must surrender original permits and file formal retirement within thirty (30) consecutive calendar days of actual operation cessation.

Prevention of Continuous Tax Accrual

Failure to file formal closure will result in the continuous statutory accrual of municipal local business taxes, regulatory renewal fees, and compounding monthly surcharges.

Surrender of Original Documents

The original Mayor's Permit plate and current-year paper permit must be presented at the Victoria BPLO counter during the final endorsement phase.
